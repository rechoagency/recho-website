What EchoMind Does NOT Do

Our Red Lines

We refuse to compromise Reddit's platform integrity or violate user trust. Here's what you'll never find in EchoMind:

No Data Scraping

We never scrape Reddit content outside official API access. All data collection respects Reddit's rate limits and Terms of Service.

No Sockpuppeting

We never create fake accounts or hide brand identity. All activity happens through clearly identified official brand accounts.

No Vote Manipulation

We never engage in vote brigading, coordinated upvoting, or any form of artificial engagement manipulation.

No Spam or Mass Posting

We never automate posting at scale or flood subreddits with promotional content. Every engagement is thoughtful and value-driven.

No Private Data Access

We only access publicly visible content. We never attempt to access private messages, user data, or restricted content.

No Deceptive Practices

We never misrepresent brand affiliation, create fake testimonials, or engage in astroturfing of any kind.
